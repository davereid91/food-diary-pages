Wednesday 18 November–Sunday 22 November lunch

Wednesday 18 November — Salmon teriyaki poke bowl

For two portions: 240 g salmon, 90 g dry rice, 160 g edamame, 2 kiwis, 120 g Wagamama teriyaki sauce, 2 garlic cloves, 1 lime, 5 g olive oil.

Cook rice and edamame separately; cook salmon in oil and warm teriyaki sauce with garlic and lime. Split every ingredient equally, one fillet and kiwi per portion. Reserve the second portion for Thursday lunch.

Per portion: ~654 kcal / ~40 g protein.

Thursday 19 November — Nando’s chicken, potatoes and corn

For two portions: 500 g chicken thigh fillets, 500 g potatoes, 200 g corn (drained if canned), 12.5 g Nando’s Medium seasoning, 5 g olive oil, 40 ml water.

Coat chicken in Nando’s seasoning, oil and water; cook thoroughly. Roast potatoes and cook corn separately. Divide equally and reserve one portion for Friday lunch.

Per portion: ~707 kcal / ~56 g protein.

Friday 20 November — Smoky fajita beef with potatoes

For two portions: 400 g lean beef mince, 500 g potatoes, 1 red pepper, 0.5 red onion, 10 g smoky fajita seasoning, 5 g olive oil, 40 ml water.

Roast potatoes. Cook beef, sliced pepper and onion with smoky fajita seasoning, oil and water; divide equally and reserve one portion for Saturday lunch.

Per portion: ~535 kcal / ~48 g protein.

Saturday 21 November — Lemon-and-herb chicken, potatoes and broccoli

For two portions: 500 g chicken thigh fillets, 500 g potatoes, 200 g broccoli, 1 25 g Nando’s Lemon & Herb seasoning packet, 40 ml water.

Mix the Lemon & Herb rub with a splash of water (40 ml planned) to provide moisture, coat the 500 g raw chicken, and cook thoroughly in a covered roasting dish following the chicken pack’s directions. Cook 500 g raw potatoes and 200 g broccoli separately. Divide equally for Saturday dinner and Sunday lunch. No separate lemon, herbs or garlic in this chicken batch.

Per portion: ~660 kcal / ~57 g protein.
