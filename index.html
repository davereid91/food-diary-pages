Tuesday 10 November — Salmon teriyaki poke bowl

For two: 240 g salmon, 160 g dry rice, 160 g edamame, 2 kiwi, 120 g teriyaki sauce, 2 garlic cloves, 1 lime, 10 g oil.

Cook rice and edamame separately. Cook salmon in oil; warm teriyaki sauce with garlic and lime juice. Give each person one salmon fillet and kiwi. Divide rice, edamame and sauce 60/40, Dave/Sarah. No pepper or onion.

Wednesday 11 November — Nandos chicken, sweet potato and corn bowl

For two: 370 g chicken, 500 g sweet potato, 2 corn cobs, 1 red pepper, 1 red onion, 12.5 g Nando’s seasoning, 10 g oil.

Coat chicken with Nandos seasoning, oil and a little water. Cook chicken thoroughly; roast sweet potato, pepper and onion, and cook corn. Divide chicken, sweet potato, pepper, onion and coating 60/40; corn one each.
